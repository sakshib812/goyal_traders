Refund Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Scope & Core Principles

At Goyal Traders, we prioritize transparent, prompt, and predictable financial reconciliation. This Refund & Cancellation Policy applies to all digital recharge transactions, utility bill payments, insurance distribution premiums, FASTag top-ups, and partner API ledger settlements processed through our platform.

Our automated switching engine implements real-time reconciliation with downstream operator switches, National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) Bharat BillPay nodes, and acquiring partner banks to ensure that no merchant or consumer is unfairly penalized for failed transactions.

Guiding Standard: All refund timelines and dispute mechanisms follow the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) circular on “Harmonisation of Turn Around Time (TAT) and customer compensation for failed transactions using authorized Payment Systems”.

2. Transaction Classification & Reversal Guidelines

Every digital payment request dispatched through Goyal Traders falls into one of three operational states:

Successful Transactions

Operator acknowledges receipt and plan credit. Once delivered, utility and airtime transactions are irrevocable and cannot be cancelled.

Immediate Failed Transactions

Operator responds with an explicit failure status code. Funds are instantly auto-reversed back to the partner ledger within seconds.

Pending / Processing Status

Downstream network latency prevents immediate confirmation. Final status is reconciled within 2 to 24 hours via automated status polling.

Duplicate Deductions

Where multiple debits occur for a single request, redundant deductions are flagged by our anti-duplication engine and reversed promptly.

3. Service-Specific Refund Mechanisms

A. Mobile & DTH Recharge Transactions

  • Incorrect Destination Number / Subscriber ID: Customers and retail agents are responsible for verifying input parameters prior to submission. In instances where an operator marks a transaction as successfully credited to the submitted number, Goyal Traders cannot reverse or recall funds due to operator regulatory limits.
  • Failed Recharges: In the event of network timeout, technical failure, or operator service downtime where funds were debited from the partner’s wallet, our auto-retry engine will either complete the delivery or trigger an automatic reversal within 15 to 30 minutes.

B. Electricity, Water, Gas & BBPS Bill Payments

  • Bill Processing Latency: Some municipal boards and electricity distribution companies (DISCOMs) require 24 to 48 hours to update their consumer-facing ledgers after receiving payment confirmation from the BBPS central switch.
  • Rejected by Biller: If a biller rejects a payment (e.g. account already paid, overdue threshold passed, or incorrect consumer ID), the entire debited amount is returned to the originating source within T+2 working days.

C. Partner Wallet & API Pre-funding Top-Ups

  • Partner wallet deposits via UPI, IMPS, NEFT, or RTGS that fail to reflect due to banking inter-switch latency will be credited automatically upon receipt of the downstream bank reconciliation file (typically within 2 to 4 business hours).

4. Turn Around Time (TAT) Matrix

The table below outlines our standard resolution and refund disbursement turnaround times:

  • Partner Virtual Wallet Reversals: Instantaneous to maximum 30 minutes for confirmed failed API requests.
  • Consumer Bank Account / UPI Reversals: 3 to 5 business days, subject to the cardholder’s issuing bank processing cycles.
  • Pending Transaction Resolution: Final status determined within 24 hours of transaction initiation.
  • Grievance Escalation Review: Acknowledged within 24 hours; closed within 3 business days.

5. How to Initiate a Refund Inquiry or Dispute

If an authorized partner, retailer, or end-consumer identifies an uncredited transaction or duplicate charge, please submit a formal inquiry with the following mandatory details:

  • Unique Goyal Traders Transaction ID or API Reference Number
  • Date and exact timestamp of the transaction
  • Consumer identifier (Mobile number, CA number, or DTH ID)
  • Exact transaction amount in INR
  • Bank reference number / UTR number (for wallet top-up debits)
