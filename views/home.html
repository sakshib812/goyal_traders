PARTNER NETWORK

Roles & Hierarchy

Our network is organised in clear levels, each with its own dashboard, permissions and earning model, so everyone knows what they handle and what they earn.

Top-level partner

Whitelabel

Runs the services under its own brand. Builds the network below it, chooses which services are switched on and sets the commission plan for every level.

What you can do:
  • ✓ Launch services under your own brand
  • ✓ Create and manage master distributors, distributors and retailers
  • ✓ Control active services and commissions
Network builder

Master Distributor

Appoints and supports distributors and retailers across a wider region, keeps their wallets funded and watches how the whole network performs.

What you can do:
  • ✓ Onboard distributors and retailers
  • ✓ Manage commissions and wallet balances
  • ✓ Track network-wide performance
Area partner

Distributor

Brings local shops onto the platform, enables the right services for them and supports them day to day. Earns on the transactions of the retailers in the area.

What you can do:
  • ✓ Onboard and train retailers
  • ✓ Assign services and pricing to retailers
  • ✓ Top up retailer wallets and view area reports
Counter partner

Retailer / Agent

Serves walk-in customers at the shop for recharges, bill payments and insurance premiums, and earns a commission on each completed transaction.

What you can do:
  • ✓ Recharge mobiles and DTH
  • ✓ Collect electricity, utility and fee payments
  • ✓ Earn commission and view your transaction history

Which roles are open for new partners may vary by region. Talk to our team to find the right fit.
