Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Overview & Introduction

Welcome to Goyal Traders (“we”, “us”, or “our”). Goyal Traders provides modular fintech APIs, digital recharge, utility bill payment switching, identity verification workflows, and banking correspondent connectivity across India.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting all personal and transactional data processed through our website, developer portals, software panels, and API endpoints. This Privacy Policy outlines our standards regarding the collection, storage, processing, transfer, and safeguarding of information under the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

Important Notice: By accessing our website, integrating our APIs, or utilizing any of our fintech services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and consented to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to deliver efficient digital transaction routing, regulatory compliance, fraud monitoring, and partner support:

A. Information Provided by You Directly

  • Contact & Identification Data: Full name, authorized representative details, work email address, mobile telephone number, and business entity name submitted via our enquiry forms or partner registration checklists.
  • Business & KYC Verification Data: Certificate of incorporation, GSTIN registration, PAN details, bank account validation credentials, and registered office addresses necessary for commercial onboarding.
  • Communication Records: Queries, technical support logs, integration correspondence, and transactional feedback.

B. Transactional & Operational API Data

  • Utility & Recharge Parameters: Mobile telephone numbers, DTH subscriber account IDs, electricity consumer numbers, water and gas connection identifiers, biller reference codes, invoice amounts, and transaction status receipts.
  • Switching Metadata: Unique transaction reference IDs (UTR), operator sequence codes, API request timestamps, IP addresses, latency metrics, and error failure diagnostics.

C. Technical & Device Information

  • Log & Connection Data: Operating system specifications, browser user-agents, IP addresses, network service providers, and session telemetry captured for network security and load balancing.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

Goyal Traders processes your information strictly for legitimate commercial, contractual, and statutory purposes:

Service Execution & API Routing

Routing recharge, bill inquiry, FASTag, and verification requests to respective telecom operators, BBPS nodes, and partner banks.

Reconciliation & Settlement

Balancing partner virtual ledger accounts, computing service commissions, generating tax invoices, and processing auto-reversals.

Security & Fraud Prevention

Detecting anomalies, mitigating velocity attacks, preventing unauthorized API key exposure, and protecting data pipelines.

Regulatory Compliance

Maintaining transaction audit trails mandated by RBI guidelines, NPCI regulations, tax authorities, and Indian cyber laws.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or consumer data to marketing third parties. We share information only with:

  • Licensed Telecom & DTH Operators: For executing live mobile and television subscriber balance renewals.
  • Bharat Bill Payment System (BBPS) & Utility Boards: For fetching billing invoices and remitting municipal and utility payments.
  • Partner Banks & Payment Aggregators: For processing nodal settlements, payout validations, and banking correspondent workflows.
  • Statutory & Law Enforcement Authorities: When compelled by valid court orders, subpoenas, or statutory reporting guidelines under Indian jurisprudence.

5. Data Protection, Security & Storage

Goyal Traders enforces multi-layered technical and organizational safeguards:

  • Encryption: Industry-standard TLS 1.3 / AES-256 bit encryption in transit and at rest for all sensitive data fields.
  • Access Control: Role-based access controls (RBAC), multi-factor authentication (MFA), and tokenized API secret keys.
  • Data Localization: All server infrastructure and transactional databases are hosted in ISO 27001 / SOC 2 certified data center facilities located physically within the Republic of India.
  • Retention: Data is retained only for the duration necessary to satisfy commercial agreements and statutory audit timelines (typically up to 7 years per regulatory guidelines).
