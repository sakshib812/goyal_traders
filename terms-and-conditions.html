2. Description of Services

Paisara operates as a fintech service switch and technology middleware platform offering:

  • Mobile Recharge API: Real-time prepaid mobile balance top-ups across supported Indian telecom operators.
  • DTH Recharge: Set-top box balance renewals with immediate operator confirmations.
  • Electricity & Bill Payments: BBPS-compliant utility bill inquiry, fetching, and remittance pipelines.
  • Utility & Fee Payments: FASTag toll recharges, gas and water billing, and institutional fee settlements.
  • Insurance Distribution: Digital policy issuance and renewal workflows with licensed insurance carriers.
  • Dedicated Partner Support: Technical integration documentation, sandbox testbeds, and operational helpdesks.
Regulatory Role: Paisara serves as a technology platform and technical service provider facilitating integration between licensed financial institutions, payment gateways, BBPS operating units, licensed telecom carriers, and downstream merchants.
